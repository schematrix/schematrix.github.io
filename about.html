Who's behind Schematrix?

Schematrix is an independent technology studio — one person designing, building, and shipping hardware and software projects end to end, without the usual product/sales layer in between.

The current focus is Meschy, an open-source mesh communication platform. Everything here is built the way it's described in the manifesto: understand it first, then simplify.
