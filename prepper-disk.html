HAS_PrepperDisk

A Raspberry Pi 5 that carries Wikipedia, maps, medical references and survival books, and serves them over its own Wi-Fi. No internet, no cloud, no account.

Offline Library

Built on Internet-in-a-Box and Kiwix, HAS_PrepperDisk keeps around 170 GB of knowledge on a 256 GB NVMe SSD: the full English and Turkish Wikipedia with images, street-level maps of Europe and the Middle East, and a curated library on water, food, first aid, repair and off-grid living.

The Pi broadcasts its own password-protected Wi-Fi network. Join it from any phone or laptop and the homepage opens by itself, no app needed. Setup scripts, the content list and the homepage are all on GitHub.

Hardware & Software

A Raspberry Pi 5 with an M.2 HAT and an NVMe SSD. The operating system runs from the SD card, all content lives on the SSD. Kiwix serves Wikipedia and the rest of the library, OpenStreetMap tiles serve the maps, and a hotspot with a captive portal turns the Pi into a self-contained network.

When the Internet Goes Down, the Knowledge Stays.

Earthquake, flood, power cut or just a cabin far from any signal: everything on HAS_PrepperDisk works with no connection at all. Plug it into a power bank and every phone nearby gets an encyclopedia, a map and a first aid manual.
